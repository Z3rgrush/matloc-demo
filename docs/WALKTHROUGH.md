# ARGUS demo: guided walkthrough

A 10 to 12 minute presenter script for the ARGUS demo. It follows the in-app guided tour step for step, so you can either press **Guided tour** and let it drive, or click through yourself using the cues below.

**Before you start**

- Open `index.html` (or the GitHub Pages URL). Press **Reset** in the top bar so the data is fresh.
- Leave "Acting as" on **Imprint marketer**.
- The demo date is **November 5, 2026**. All titles and numbers are fictional.

**The one idea to land:** *the agent proposes, the human disposes.* The AI does the watching and the drafting; ordinary code enforces the guardrails; people make every decision that touches money, targeting or creative.

## Likely questions

**Does the AI ever change a live campaign by itself?**
No. The only automatic action is the emergency pause on a hard-cap breach (§7.4), which can be turned off, only reduces spend, and is one click to reverse. Section 14, question 12 asks leadership to confirm this exception.

**What happens if nobody approves?**
Proposals expire (three days in the demo) because stale evidence leads to bad actions. Press **Advance one day** a few times to watch one expire.

**Can we trust the numbers?**
Platform numbers are labeled as platform-reported. ARGUS keeps its own spend ledger, reconciles daily against platform data and monthly against invoices, and never presents attributed revenue as incremental.

**Why Meta and Amazon first?**
Amazon has the cleanest attribution and Meta has the most mature API. Google and TikTok follow because of access gating (§6, §12).

**What does the AI actually do here?**
In production the Planner, Optimizer and Reporter use an LLM with read-only tools to draft plans, proposals and narratives. Its output is validated against a schema and then the policy engine. In this demo those roles are seeded heuristics so the story is repeatable.

## Free exploration ideas

- Switch "Acting as" to **Marketing director** and see which cards unlock.
- On Budget & pacing, turn **auto-pause off** and simulate the spike: you get a critical alert but no pause.
- Turn on the **kill switch** and try approving anything.
- Lower the Watch threshold to 5% and see more titles move to Watch.
- Reject a proposal and pick reason tags; they appear in the audit log.
